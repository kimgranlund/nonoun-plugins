## Decisions (T-0893)

Measured on a cream page `#fff3e4` with ink `#2a1608`, steps 1 to 4, before and after `pageTint: 100`:

| Family | Mode | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| cyan | light | `#f8f8f9 #e6f0f5 #d3e7f0 #c0dce8` | `#fff3e4 #eeebe1 #dce2dd #c8d7d7` |
| orange | light | `#fff6f0 #ffeadb #feddc6 #f8cfb1` | `#fff3e4 #fee8d1 #fadbbe #f4cdaa` |
| cyan | dark | `#0d1214 #151f24 #1f3138 #28424c` | `#2a1608 #302217 #38322c #3f4341` |
| orange | dark | `#16100b #281b12 #3d2a1c #533825` | `#2a1608 #381e0c #4a2b15 #5e391e` |

1. Weight curve: a lightness weight on the tone ladder from the contrast targets, the same for every family. A per-family OKLab L weight lost all tint from step 3 on pinned light or dark solids. Against fading by APCA target, it keeps more tint in light steps 6 to 8 and less in dark; the two differ by at most ΔE 0.13 in light and 0.54 in dark.
2. Strength: a number in `settings` (the user's addition). Dark cyan at 100 is brown-grey in steps 2 to 4; with `modes.dark.settings.pageTint: 50` it is `#2a1608 #262421 #2f3434 #374448`, and the hue shows by step 3.
3. With `neutrals`: accents re-base on the ground, which is the page and the surface the alpha steps are solved over.
4. Alpha steps, series and tonal map: re-solved from the moved ramps; accent a1 becomes transparent. The tonal scale is seeded from the solids, so `tonalRoles` stops carry no page tint (documented).
5. Tone gap: with the key on, accents are placed against the anchored page's tone, rounded to the 0.5 settings grid. Without it, a `WARNING` and no output change, so existing anchored output does not move silently.

## Upgrade notes

1. Update `design@nonoun`, then `/reload-plugins` or start a new session.
2. Output is unchanged unless a request sets `settings.pageTint`. A request with a `ground` or `ink` anchor whose page tone differs from `toneMax` or `darkTone` by more than 0.5 now prints a `WARNING` line; its files are unchanged.
