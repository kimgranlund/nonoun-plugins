## Prices

`prices.json` keeps the `haiku` key with its numbers (0.21 blended, 0.58 usage_input). It is unrefit: both rates were set on Haiku 4.5, the list price fell from $1 input and $5 output to $0.10 and $0.50 per MTok (prompts up to 100K tokens; $0.50 and $2.50 above; cache read $0.01, cache write 5m $0.125 and 1h $0.20), and the newer tokenizer counts about 30 percent more tokens for the same text. The one recorded 5.5 run below estimated $0.034094 against a host figure of $0.00559 (about 6x over), too few rows to fit a rate. Haiku cost estimates overstate until a refit.
