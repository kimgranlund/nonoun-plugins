The session designs the retry policy for the webhook sender, to be written to `docs/design/webhook-retry.md`. Decided so far: exponential backoff with jitter, 8 attempts, a base delay of 10s, and failed events stored in the `dead_events` table. A Redis queue was considered and rejected. Sections 1 and 2 are drafted; section 3 on dead events is next. Open: the delay ceiling, and who replays dead events (pending a talk with ops).
