## Upgrade notes

1. Adopt 0.22.5 and run `/reload-plugins`. A Codex repository regenerates its agents with `codex_roles.py install`.
2. The light merge lane and `settle --forward` are opt-in: nothing changes until a repo declares `merge_gate: light` and `light_scope` in `.sdlc/config`. A repo needs its owner's approval before loosening full gate before fast-forward.
3. The gate lock capacity is a machine setting in `~/.local/state/sdlc-lite/gate-lock/policy.json` (`gate_lock.py policy set`), not a plugin default. The built-in capacity is unchanged at 6 compute units.
4. Nothing from 0.22.4 is removed.
