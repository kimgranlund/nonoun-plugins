The merged contract. `SKILL.md` is a router plus the shared procedure, at most 90 lines and 7 KB (today the five bodies total 170 lines and each loads alone). ## Rejected alternatives
- Keep seven skills and share only `plan-rules.md`: removes the 45 duplicated lines but keeps five competing descriptions, three check items pinned to five files, and five places to add each new rule; and no evidence says the names misroute today, so this alternative is the cheapest if the maintainer values per-call context over one contract.
- Merge only plan-goal and plan-campaign into plan-doc: a campaign returns a `/goal` block and a lane partition, not a plan doc with front matter, approval and `plan_done.py`; forcing them into the doc shape changes what they produce and leaves `plan` and `plan-fanout` outside the merge.
- A plan-doc-only world with no `/plan goal`: the pasted `/goal` block is the cheap overnight path (the 0.20.8 goal, `goal-rules.md:11`) and the campaign's output; a plan doc costs front matter, steps, approval and a driver, and `run-plan` is a declared non-change.
- Rename `plan_check.py` kinds to one `plan` kind with views: collides with the pinned plan-doc kind (item 49, spec 3.3, run-plan preflight, `plan_done.py`); a `--view` alias would add a second name for the same checker with no consumer.
- Merge `plan-loop` too: a `/loop` line is not a plan, has its own parser and lint (`check_loop`, `:192`), and the handoff names it unchanged.
- Delete the old folders in ticket A: breaks items 48 and 51 and 45 spec references in the same ticket as the new skill; stubs separate the additive step from the retarget.

## Risks
- Description routing: one description must carry the trigger phrases of five; if it reads as a steps-only skill, "write me a goal" may route to `/goal` planning by the host or nowhere. The evals replay is the only check, and it is not a host routing test.
- The widened `allowed-tools` grants `Write`, `Edit` and `Agent` on kinds that only read; a kind-specific guard is instruction text, not enforcement.
- If T-0461 renames the clarify slots, the slot reading in step 1 reads nothing and every kind falls back to questions; A must re-read the built clarify skill.
- A `--summary` cost line summed from `budget.py` TIERS reports a cap, not spend; readers may take it as a forecast.
- The 90-line target may not hold once the kind-resolution and the slot table are written; if it reaches 130 lines the per-call cost argument against the merge strengthens and the first alternative should be reconsidered.
- The controlled-language warnings on goal blocks rewrite the example in `goal.md`; the campaign template and worked example may also fail `verb` and need rewording, which widens C.
- A scratch copy of this design was written to `/tmp/0462/` for format validation and its removal was denied by the permission system; it holds nothing the repository needs and can be deleted by the maintainer.
