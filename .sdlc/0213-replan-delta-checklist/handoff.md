## Goal
Make a replan cheap: a scripted replan handoff, a planner checklist from the findings corpus, a scoped plan review, and a `replans` metric.

## Intent
- Do: Section C. `steps.py replan-handoff <task dir>` is the scripted form of the chain's `plan review: replan: <finding>` append into handoff.md (not a second file); it refuses when no `replan` verdict counts after the newest plan. `agents/planner.md` gains a checklist, one line per finding class with a dated citation (every design row or goal clause has a criterion; every untagged criterion is red at the start of its step; every called function is named with its failure input; every new file is in the layout record that enumerates files; a slow guard has a `guard timeout:` line). `agents/plan-reviewer.md`: after `specgate.py check` passes review only judgment items, and a re-review after a delta reads T-0200's merged view and the findings it answers. `footprint.py` gains `replans` (non-stale planner results beyond the first plus replan verdicts). The existing stop after two replan verdicts stays and the conductor's hard stop is T-0199 text (d). Regenerate Codex agents. Authorized model and effort change (the user explicitly approved it on 2026-10-04; no other default changes): a plan-reviewer run that is a delta re-review (the task dir holds an earlier plan-reviewer result with Verdict replan and the plan under review is a replan of it) runs one effort level below the plan-reviewer's current effort, `medium` instead of `high`, on whichever family the existing family rule picks. Implement it in `scripts/run.sh` (the plan-reviewer case beside the family rule, with the reason recorded in the run line's routing_reason) and in `skills/level-rubric/SKILL.md`; `scripts/check.py` must keep checking their agreement, with a negative control that a rubric/run.sh mismatch fails. Under the T-0185 model-table lock rule, add a new eval file under `.sdlc/evals/` (`evals.py add`) and update the lock with `scripts/evals.py lock`; the eval records honestly what it measured, namely the replay of delta re-reviews on the four recorded replans and the live T-0213 evidence (whether each delta re-review at medium still named the findings it was checking and passed or sent back the right plans), the model, effort, host, task set, pass rate (passed over total of those re-reviews), measured cost, and evidence paths, and states plainly that it is a small sample and not a benchmark. Run `evals.py check` and the lock check as gates. If the recorded re-reviews at medium miss a finding that the high-effort review caught on the same plan, stop and ask the user instead of merging the change.
- Non-goals: any model or effort change other than the authorized delta re-review effort above; conductor wording beyond a pointer line; a version bump, tag, push, or publish.
- Done when: the changes are built, an independent verifier passed each step, and selftest for replan-handoff on a seeded task dir with a replan verdict and a negative control that it refuses without one; footprint selftest for replans including a stale rerun; replay: the checklist as a rubric on the four replanned plans names at least half the judgment findings, otherwise the checklist is removed and the ticket reports it. `bash plugins/sdlc-lite/scripts/check.sh`, `python3 scripts/check-packaging.py`, `python3 plugins/sdlc-lite/scripts/codex_roles.py generate --check` and `python3 plugins/sdlc-lite/scripts/tickets.py --root .sdlc check` pass, and affected guidance is updated.
