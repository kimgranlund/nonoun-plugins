## Goal
A restarted messenger surfaces messages already claimed (in read/<receiver>) but not acknowledged, once, and reports "N new; M previously claimed remain unacknowledged" instead of "no new messages".

## Intent
- Do: change the messenger loop (plugins/sdlc-lite/agents/messenger.md, then `codex_roles.py generate` and `claude_roles.py generate` for the generated text, and the mailbox protocol text in .sdlc/messages/AGENTS.md as generated by messages.py or onboard) and the CLI support it needs in scripts/messages.py. On startup or resume the messenger lists new/<receiver> and read/<receiver> for its own receiver only. New messages are claimed with `messages.py read` as today. Previously claimed messages are reported in a separate section with id, sender, topic, send_time, read_time and follow_up_requested; the parent decides what to do; the messenger never replays, replies to or acknowledges them on its own. Report status precisely, for example "0 new messages; 10 previously claimed messages remain unacknowledged." Do not change `wait` to return read messages on every poll (that would loop notifications); if a CLI aid is needed, add a read-only `messages.py backlog --receiver NAME [--since-marker ID]` or reuse `list --state read` and keep the once-only behavior in the messenger (for example a `read/<receiver>/.backlog-reported` marker or reporting only on the first poll after spawn).
- Non-goals: model or effort changes; changing `wait` semantics; acknowledging or deleting old messages.
- Done when:
  - A selftest case: empty new queue and a nonempty read queue surfaces the backlog once; a second poll does not repeat it (observed failing first).
  - A message for another receiver is never claimed or listed; a new arrival is still claimed and forwarded; ack and follow-up rules are unchanged (existing cases pass).
  - messages.py selftest, check.py, tickets.py check, both generate --check pass.

## Context
Codex report on SDLC Lite 0.21.3, repo ui-kit-maison, 2026-10-08: a restarted Codex messenger polled only new/codex and said no messages while read/codex held 10 claimed, unacknowledged messages from October 4 (new/conductor held a correctly excluded handshake). wait behaves as documented; the gap is the messenger's startup reconciliation and its status wording. The messenger is the Haiku 5.5 row on Claude and gpt-5.6-terra on Codex, so keep the instruction short and checkable.
