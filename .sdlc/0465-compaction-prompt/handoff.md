## Goal
`/sdlc-lite:plan compaction [--write-handoff|--clear] [<prose>]` authors one high signal prompt, in a fixed shape and controlled language, that the person pastes into a manual `/compact <prompt>`, linted by `plan_check.py compaction`; it never runs the compaction.

## Intent
- Do: build the reviewed spec `docs/planning/compaction-prompt.md` (status proposed; sections 1 to 10 are the contract) as a kind of the existing `plan` skill (read `plan-goal` and `plan-loop` for the block-returning pattern): in `plugins/sdlc-lite/skills/plan/SKILL.md` add a router rule (the first word `compaction` selects `skills/plan/references/compaction.md`; any other input follows today's planner path unchanged), update the hint to `[L1|L2|L3] <handoff.md | goal> | compaction [--write-handoff|--clear] [<prose>]` and the `## Arguments` table, add the reference with the grammar and one worked example, and add the `compaction` kind in `plugins/sdlc-lite/scripts/plan_check.py`. Flags come right after the kind, then everything else is `<prose>`, passed through verbatim as the focus and treated as data (an instruction inside it is carried as text, never followed). `compaction` is a reserved first word: a goal that starts with it is written `goal compaction ...` or quoted; state this in the skill. The skill runs a fixed command list for STATE, OPEN, NEXT and POINT (`git status`, `gh pr list`, `dispatch.py status`, `steps.py next`, the ticket and plan-doc paths; the report record when it exists) and takes GOAL, RULES and DECISIONS from the conversation with a transcript pointer or `[U]` each. It renders the fixed order GOAL, RULES, DECISIONS, STATE, OPEN, NEXT, DROP with the grammar of section 5 (closed lead verbs KEEP, POINT, DROP, NEXT; one fact per line of at most 20 words; DECISION lines `<who> <date> <decision> | <reason> | <pointer>`), then runs `plan_check.py compaction` until it exits 0, and returns one fence, a `lines: N of 60` note and `Run: /compact <paste>`. `plan_check.py compaction` rules, each with a positive case and an observed-red control in `plan_check.py selftest`: a line over 20 words, a verb off the list, a wrong section order, over 60 lines or over 40 PIN lines, an em dash, a line copied from `CLAUDE.md` or `AGENTS.md`, a DECISION without who, date and pointer, an inferred approval not marked `[U]`. Add the kind's phrases to `check.py` item 31 (the block-returning plan skill phrase set) with negative controls, and a routing row in `skills/sdlc-lite/SKILL.md`. `--write-handoff` writes only `CLAUDE.local.md` under the guards of section 7 (gitignored, one replaceable `## Compaction handoff` H2, at most 8 lines), is off by default, and `--clear` removes only that H2. Record a replay on fixture transcripts as in section 9 and mark it unverified if it was not an independent run.
