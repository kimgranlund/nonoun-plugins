# Generative stress evaluation, 2026-10-08

Exploratory evaluation, not controlled efficacy proof. Two task/model pairings: gpt-5.6-terra grocery prototype and gpt-6.1-sol elaborate component catalog. Fresh-context rounds 1, 2 and 4; round 3 explicitly supervised repair. Separate model-assisted evaluator inspected Chromium DOM, screenshots and interactions. No blind human ratings or model superiority inference. Different tasks and one attempt per condition limit conclusions.

Evidence workspace: /tmp/wireframe-generative-eval-GfQS3O. First passes, source snapshots, retrieval logs, reports and screenshots retained there. Whole-file retrieval metrics are not actual assembled-prompt measurements.

Confirmed lessons:

- Structural/schema success did not detect unreadable contextual paragraph text, clipped selects, blank header space or inert quantity controls.
- Both fresh generators invented plausible token names. The lexical no-fallback reference preflight catches this repeatable failure; injected missing reference rejects and restored reference passes. It does not validate contrast or cascade.
- Contextual foreground guidance improved Sol's inverse surface on fresh Round2 and Round4.
- Explicit foundation guidance improved Terra's dark switching in Round2 but did not prevent undefined roles, composition and interaction regressions.
- Supervised repairs produced better artifacts, but that is not evidence of autonomous reliability.
- Sol Round4 fresh output has valid references and readable contextual surfaces, but remaining disclosure alignment and menu-gap defects. Further prose alone has not ensured component-level consistency.
- Grocery supervised Round3 restores compact header, sequential layout, quantity edits and legible delivery selection. Static flow boundaries remain explicit.

Adopted narrowly: contextual foreground/slot guidance; foundation consumption and fragment composition reminders; token-reference preflight in build checklist. No new ontology/manifests added. Next candidate improvement is a tested reusable navigation/overlay recipe plus a rendered control-fit check, validated on new tasks rather than adding more paragraphs indiscriminately.

Final fresh Terra Round4 retained two high failures: literal palette remains light in dark scheme, and quantity button is inert despite editable-basket claims. Layout, routes and token-reference checks pass. Final fresh Sol has no high blocker but still misses disclosure alignment and menu spacing. Stopped at four rounds; overall unattended-generation reliability is not accepted. Eight artifacts total: six fresh-context generations and two supervised repairs. Next checks should test literal color overrides/rendered scheme response and actual interaction promises; then compare one tested nav recipe on new tasks with swapped model/task assignments.
