# Model brief

Use only the fields needed for this task; keep this record with the local model.

- **Decision and users:** What operation should this knowledge support?
- **Scope:** Included domain, sources, contexts and relevant versions; excluded work.
- **Existing authority:** Vocabulary, contracts, owners and consumers to reuse.
- **Competency cases:** A supported question, boundary and unknown case with evidence.
- **Representation:** Why the chosen document/table/schema/graph is sufficient.
- **Identities and relations:** Needed distinctions; link their definitions instead
  of duplicating them.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Source-native claims, derivations, observations and
  unresolved interpretations.
- **Ownership:** Authored inputs, generated views and any implemented validation.
- **Acceptance:** Actual decisions/consumer checks and their observed results.
- **Compatibility and limits:** Historical interpretation, affected consumers and
  unsupported conclusions.
