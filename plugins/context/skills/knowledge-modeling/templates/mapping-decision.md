# Mapping decision

- **Purpose:** Search, comparison, transformation or other intended operation.
- **Source identity:** Native concept/property and source/model version.
- **Target identity:** Canonical or other native concept/property and version.
- **Correspondence:** Direction and precise relation; unresolved alternatives if needed.
- **Scope and preconditions:** Context, units, release, population or other relevant limits.
- **Evidence:** Definitions, excerpts/locators, method and rationale.
- **Uncertainty:** What remains unknown; a score only if its meaning is justified.
- **Decision status:** Candidate/adopted/rejected or the project's actual lifecycle.
- **Consumer eligibility:** Which policy permits the requested use; which uses remain unsupported.
- **Verification:** Supported example, misleading neighbor and actual consumer result.
- **History:** Prior mapping, supersession and preservation of retained interpretations.

Keep the native assertions intact. Complete a mapping record only to the degree
supported by evidence; do not fill gaps with invented values or authority.
